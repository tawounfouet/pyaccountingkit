# ADR-CC-08C - Privileged intent reconciliation and authorization revocation (candidate)

- **Status:** PROPOSED. This record is not approved and cannot authorize a production deployment.
- **Context:** Privileged PostgreSQL role mutation cannot be committed atomically with a separately durable security outcome audit and backend termination. A crash can leave committed intent without trustworthy terminal evidence. A policy/approval check made before intent is also subject to time-of-check/time-of-use races.
- **Proposed decision:** An independent security reviewer must explicitly authorize tenant-scoped identification of aged intents without terminal outcome. Write only immutable `OUTCOME_MISSING / UNKNOWN` evidence. **Never infer SUCCEEDED/FAILED or replay a privileged action automatically.** Use row-level locking and uniqueness to prevent duplicate review records. Add second independently resolved approval/policy check before DDL; cancelled checks produce terminal `CANCELLED` evidence without executing DDL.
- **Consequences:** Puts bounded work into an owner-only review query. Nonterminal reviews may coexist with later terminal evidence because they capture a historically true observation. Incident resolution must use separate, approved actions and independent evidence. The second check narrows, but cannot eliminate, IAM revocation races.
- **Alternatives rejected for this increment:** auto-success based on `rolcanlogin` or password-hash state; retrying `ALTER ROLE` on stale intent; converting missing outcome to FAILED; trusting client-supplied actor/approval dataclasses as proof; claiming `CANCELLED` after a role operation has run.
- **Further decisions:** independent control-plane IAM, review retention and tamper-evidence/WORM, escrow and evidence integrity, exact transaction/lease protocol, incident ownership and policy on late audit, protected owner privileges, and reconciler scheduling.
